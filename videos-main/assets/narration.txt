# Target: ~150 words, 72 seconds at ~125 wpm
# Voice: confident, warm, calm. Pauses between sentences are intentional.
# Each block maps to one scene.

# Scene 1 — Pain hook (0-4s) | 9 words, 4s with pause
You apply to a hundred jobs.
You hear back from three.

# Scene 2 — Brand reveal (4-8.5s) | 8 words, 4.5s with pause
Resume AI changes that.

# Scene 3 — Dashboard land (8.5-17s) | 22 words, 8.5s
Your candidate workspace.
Resumes, applications, AI matching — every part of the search lives in one place.

# Scene 4 — Job preferences (17-25s) | 15 words, 8s
Tell it what you want.
Roles. Locations. Compensation. The fit math runs on every job, automatically.

# Scene 5 — ATS Optimizer (25-37s) | 22 words, 12s
Drop in any job posting.
The ATS optimizer scores your resume in seconds —
then rewrites it to win the keywords you're missing.

# Scene 6 — Recommended jobs (37-45s) | 12 words, 8s
It surfaces the roles you'd actually take.
Ranked by real fit, not noise.

# Scene 7 — Chrome extension (45-58s) | 19 words, 13s
And the Chrome extension applies for you.
On LinkedIn. With the right resume. Even while you sleep.

# Scene 8 — Payoff stats (58-66s) | 12 words, 8s
Forty-seven applications a week.
Six interviews this month.
Zero hours lost.

# Scene 9 — CTA (66-72s) | 4 words, 6s
Resume AI. Start free.
